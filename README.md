# Employee Attrition Analysis 

## Overview

Employee attrition is one of the major challenges faced by organizations. Understanding the reasons why employees leave can help companies improve employee satisfaction, reduce turnover, and enhance workforce productivity.

This project performs an in-depth analysis of employee attrition using Python. Various factors such as employee satisfaction, salary, workload, promotions, departments, and working hours are analyzed to identify the key drivers of employee turnover.

---

## Problem Statement

Employee turnover can negatively impact organizational performance, productivity, and recruitment costs. The objective of this project is to analyze employee data and identify the factors that contribute to employees leaving the company.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Identify factors affecting employee retention.
- Study the impact of salary on employee turnover.
- Analyze employee satisfaction levels.
- Examine the relationship between workload and attrition.
- Investigate the effect of promotions on retention.
- Generate actionable business insights.

## Key Findings

- Employees with lower satisfaction levels are more likely to leave the company.
- Low salary employees exhibit higher attrition rates.
- Employees working longer hours tend to leave more frequently.
- Excessive workload contributes to employee turnover.
- Lack of promotions negatively affects employee retention.
- Certain departments experience higher attrition compared to others.
- Employee satisfaction is a critical factor influencing retention.

---

## Business Insights

- Employees with lower satisfaction levels are more likely to leave the company.
- Employees receiving lower salaries show higher attrition rates.
- Employees working longer hours tend to leave the organization more frequently.
- A higher number of assigned projects may contribute to employee turnover.
- Lack of promotions negatively impacts employee retention.
- Certain departments experience higher attrition rates compared to others.
- These insights can help improve HR management practices and employee retention strategies.

---

## Conclusion

This project successfully identified the major factors influencing employee attrition. The analysis revealed that employee satisfaction, salary, workload, working hours, promotions, and department-specific conditions significantly impact employee turnover.

The insights derived from this analysis can help organizations improve employee retention, enhance workplace satisfaction, and make informed HR decisions.
